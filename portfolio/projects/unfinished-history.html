About this project

A 2D pixel-art action platformer built in C# with the MonoGame framework. Made by a team of three.

The game contain tutorial prologue and three levels, each ending in a boss fight. Player can run, jump, dash, climb ladders and vines, in last level player also beable to swing across gaps with a grappling hook, while fighting with fists, soul bullet. Player have soul as resource to use bullet,dash, glide and grappling hook.

This project inspire Terraria, using song from Eternity's Divide OST

What I did

Enemies and AI

I've built the base enemy class and its state-based AI: idle, patrol, chase and line-of-sight detection.

I create enemy multiple enemy including Skeleton, Hellhound (spots the player, charges and jumps over obstacles), Slime, Demon and Tower that shooting bullet to player.

All 3 bosses

I also create 3 bosses Giant Slime, Cerberus and Rhulk, each with its own AI and attack patterns. I also fixed exploits on boss, such as standing next to a wall to dodge every attack.

Projectiles and level mechanics

- Projectile base class used by every bullet, Soul bullet ,enemy fireball and laser

- grappling hook

- soul minions on staff

- tutorial signs.

Menus, sound and music

The pause, settings, game over and game clear screens with volume sliders, every enemy and boss sound effect, music for each stage, and final item balancing.
