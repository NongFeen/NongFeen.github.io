About this project

A 2D multiplayer top-down RPG made with Unity, with controller support. Inspired by Destiny 2 and Soul Knight.

Semifinalist in the Central Region of the 2026 National Software Contest (NSC), Thailand.

Using A* pathfinding from arongranberg for enemy AI

All weapons and relics generate with random stats

Built data driven skills and buffs system for easier in development

Server-authoritative for projectiles with object pooling

5 playable classes

13 skills across all classes, with a level cap of 30.

5 weapons

3 relic rarities

8 enemies, including 2 bosses

8 levels

Including a co-op-only raid level, inspired by Crota's bridge encounter from Destiny 2.
